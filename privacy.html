Codhaven: privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Codhaven is a game for LG webOS TVs, made by TM Moniruzzaman Sunny.

Codhaven does not collect, send or share any personal information.

  • The game doesn't connect to the internet. It has no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress (money, catches, journal, gear, your pet) and your settings are saved only on your TV, in the app's own storage. Removing the app removes them.
  • The game reads the Magic Remote's motion sensor while you play, to cast and to look around. This stays on the TV and isn't stored.
  • Nothing is shared with anyone: there is nothing to share.

The LG Content Store, which you get the game from, has its own privacy policy from LG.
